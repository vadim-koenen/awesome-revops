# Awesome RevOps [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of tools, communities, playbooks, and reading material for revenue operations — the working layer where marketing, sales, and customer success operate as one system.

Every link here has been used in a real engagement or evaluated against one. The list is opinionated. If a category has only one entry, it is because everything else I have used in that category was not good enough to recommend.

## Marketing automation platforms

- [Marketo Engage](https://business.adobe.com/products/marketo/adobe-marketo.html) — the enterprise default for B2B. Strong on programs, smart campaigns, tokens, and integrations. Weaker on UX. The platform that rewards operational discipline more than any other.
- [HubSpot Marketing Hub](https://www.hubspot.com/products/marketing) — the SMB-to-mid-market default. UI-first. Quick to stand up; harder to scale to enterprise complexity.
- [Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement (Pardot)](https://www.salesforce.com/marketing/account-engagement/) — Salesforce-native marketing automation. Strong for orgs already deep on Salesforce.
- [Iterable](https://iterable.com/) — strong for cross-channel lifecycle and consumer-style B2B motions.
- [Customer.io](https://customer.io/) — event-driven messaging. Often the right tool when product activity is the primary signal.

## CRM

- [Salesforce Sales Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/) — the default enterprise CRM. Treat it as a database, not a process tool.
- [HubSpot CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm) — strong fit for HubSpot-native orgs and for sub-200-person teams.

## ABM and intent data

- [6sense](https://6sense.com/) — predictive ABM with intent data. Strong on buying-stage classification when configured well.
- [Demandbase](https://www.demandbase.com/) — the other major predictive ABM platform.
- [Bombora](https://bombora.com/) — third-party intent data. Often surfaced inside 6sense and Demandbase as a contributing signal.
- [G2 Buyer Intent](https://www.g2.com/products/g2-buyer-intent/take-tour) — first-party signals from G2 category pages and product profiles.
- [TrustRadius Intent](https://www.trustradius.com/vendor-products/intent-data) — similar pattern from TrustRadius.

## Sales engagement

- [Salesloft](https://salesloft.com/) — sales engagement and rep workflow. Industry default for outbound.
- [Outreach](https://www.outreach.io/) — the other half of the duopoly.
- [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/) — sales engagement with built-in prospecting database. Strong fit for smaller teams.

## Data enrichment and contact discovery

- [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/) — large B2B contact and company database.
- [Cognism](https://www.cognism.com/) — strong international coverage, particularly EMEA.
- [Clay](https://www.clay.com/) — orchestration platform for data enrichment waterfalls and outbound research. The current darling for GTM engineers.
- [Unify](https://www.unifygtm.com/) — orchestration platform with similar capabilities, AI-first positioning.
- [Cargo](https://getcargo.io/) — orchestration platform, GTM engineer-friendly.
- [FullEnrich](https://www.fullenrich.com/) — data enrichment waterfall as a service.
- [Lusha](https://www.lusha.com/) — contact discovery, often used as a waterfall source.
- [Hunter](https://hunter.io/) — email finder, often used as a waterfall source.

## Routing, scoring, and orchestration

- [LeanData](https://leandata.com/) — lead-to-account matching and routing. Industry default for round-robin and account-based routing.
- [Chili Piper](https://www.chilipiper.com/) — inbound routing and meeting scheduling.
- [Distribution Engine](https://www.distributionengine.com/) — Salesforce-native routing.
- [MadKudu](https://www.madkudu.com/) — predictive scoring layer.

## Reporting and attribution

- [Bizible (Adobe Marketo Measure)](https://business.adobe.com/products/marketo/marketo-measure.html) — multi-touch attribution at the campaign-touch level.
- [Dreamdata](https://dreamdata.io/) — B2B attribution focused on activity-based modeling.
- [HockeyStack](https://hockeystack.com/) — pipeline analytics and attribution, lighter-weight than Bizible.
- [Tableau](https://www.tableau.com/) — general-purpose BI.
- [Looker](https://cloud.google.com/looker) — general-purpose BI, strong fit for warehouse-native analytics.

## Communities

- [RevGenius](https://www.revgenius.com/) — Slack community for RevOps, marketing ops, and GTM systems. Largest active community in the space.
- [Pavilion](https://www.joinpavilion.com/) — gated community for revenue leaders. Strong cohort programming.
- [MarketingOps.com](https://marketingops.com/) — community and resources for marketing operations practitioners.
- [Wizards of Ops](https://www.wizopscommunity.com/) — community focused on sales operations and RevOps.
- [Modern Sales Pros](https://modernsalespros.com/) — peer community for sales operations leaders.

## Newsletters and publications

- [Sales Hacker](https://www.saleshacker.com/) — sales operations and modern sales tactics. Founded by Max Altschuler.
- [The GTM Newsletter](https://www.gtmnow.com/newsletter/) — RevOps and GTM strategy.
- [Demand Curve](https://www.demandcurve.com/) — growth marketing tactics; relevant to demand gen practitioners.
- [Reforge](https://www.reforge.com/) — product, growth, and marketing programs and writing.
- [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/) — product-led growth, applicable for PLG-influenced RevOps.
- [Crossing the Chasm](https://www.linkedin.com/newsletters/crossing-the-chasm-7234058091898781697/) — Geoffrey Moore's ongoing writing on B2B GTM.

## Books and essays

- *Predictable Revenue* — Aaron Ross. The book that codified the SDR-AE split and outbound as a discipline.
- *From Impossible to Inevitable* — Aaron Ross and Jason Lemkin. RevOps thinking at scale.
- *The Sales Acceleration Formula* — Mark Roberge. Process-first sales leadership.
- *Crossing the Chasm* — Geoffrey Moore. The original framework for B2B GTM by market stage.
- *Obviously Awesome* — April Dunford. Positioning as a RevOps input.
- *Hacking Growth* — Sean Ellis and Morgan Brown. Growth operations playbook.

## Playbooks and templates (open source)

- [marketo-audit-checklist](https://github.com/vadim-koenen/marketo-audit-checklist) — a practical, field-tested checklist for auditing a Marketo instance.
- [revops-scoping-questions](https://github.com/vadim-koenen/revops-scoping-questions) — the questions to ask before quoting a RevOps engagement.
- [marketo-naming-conventions](https://github.com/vadim-koenen/marketo-naming-conventions) — working naming conventions for Marketo programs, campaigns, emails, landing pages, forms, fields, and tokens.
- [scoring-model-design-patterns](https://github.com/vadim-koenen/scoring-model-design-patterns) — working patterns for behavior and fit scoring in B2B marketing automation.
- [6sense-marketo-routing-rules](https://github.com/vadim-koenen/6sense-marketo-routing-rules) — routing rules and orchestration patterns for teams running 6sense alongside Marketo and Salesforce.

## Conferences and events

- [Marketo Summit / Adobe Summit](https://summit.adobe.com/) — Adobe's annual conference, Marketo track is the practitioner draw.
- [Dreamforce](https://www.salesforce.com/dreamforce/) — Salesforce's flagship event.
- [SaaStr Annual](https://www.saastrannual.com/) — broad SaaS audience, strong RevOps and GTM tracks.
- [INBOUND](https://www.inbound.com/) — HubSpot's annual conference, marketing operations content alongside the strategy programming.
- [MOps-Apalooza](https://mopsapalooza.com/) — practitioner-run marketing operations conference.

## Contributing

Pull requests welcome. To add a resource, open a PR with:

1. The resource added to the appropriate section, in alphabetical order within that section.
2. A one-sentence description of why it belongs here — what the resource is good at, not just what it claims to do.
3. A link to documentation or to a representative use of the resource.

Adds without context will be closed. The list exists to be useful, not exhaustive.
